FAQs

Does it cost anything?

No. The site is free. The only cost is the ride itself, which the group splits.

Does it book the ride or handle money?

No. It does not provide drivers, rides, or payments. One person books the ride in their own app, and everyone pays their share directly.

What if nobody joins my trip?

Take the ride alone or change your plans. Nothing is charged or cancelled automatically.

Who can see my email?

This prototype does not store or show your email. It only checks that the domain ends in unc.edu.

Can someone without a UNC email post?

Not in this version. The domain check is the only verification built so far, so it does not confirm who is behind an address.

Is it safe to ride with someone I don't know?

Meet at the pickup spot you picked, tell a friend the trip details, and skip any ride that feels wrong.

When do I find out the exact pickup spot?

After you send a ride-along request, the exact spot shows on that trip. Before that, you see only the general area, such as Student Union or Franklin Street.

Is this a real service yet?

It is a prototype. Trips and requests save only on the device you use them on.

Privacy

Trips and requests are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server in this version. Your email is checked for the unc.edu domain and is not stored or shown.

Terms and support

This is a student prototype with no warranty. Meet in a public place, share your plans with someone, and pay your share directly. There is no support inbox yet.
