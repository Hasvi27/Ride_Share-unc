Stats

The problem work behind Ride Share: who we are designing for, what we are asking, and how we compare with the alternatives.

How might we

  1. How might we help students find rides?
  2. How might we make off-campus transportation cheaper for students?
  3. How might we verify that students are actually UNC students?
  4. How might we make trying something new feel easier and more natural than sticking to old habits?
  5. How might we meet people where they already are instead of asking them to adopt a brand-new routine?

User profile

Demographic
UNC students who regularly need to travel off campus, conveniently and cheaply.
  • All students, across genders, freshman through senior.
  • Underclassmen are the most likely to lack a car on campus, so they are the core group.
Psychographic
Hypothesis: students are willing to ride with someone new when they see other UNC students on the board, a clear price, and a fixed meeting spot. Trust and low effort matter more than speed.

User journey

A UNC student without a car travels to RDU Airport for Thanksgiving break and wants to split an Uber or Lyft fare with students heading the same way.

  1. Need

    Realizes they need transportation to RDU Airport.

  2. Search

    Checks GroupMe, asks friends, and looks for other students traveling that day.

  3. Compare

    Checks Uber and Lyft prices and considers splitting the fare.

  4. Match

    Opens the board and finds students leaving around the same time.

Empathy map

Says

“Uber is too expensive when I’m traveling alone.” “I wish I knew who else was going to the airport.” “I’ve asked my friends, but nobody seems to be going at the same time.”

Thinks

“There must be other UNC students going to the same destination.” “I wonder if I can find someone with a similar departure time.” “Would I feel comfortable sharing a ride with someone I don’t know?”

Does

Checks GroupMe and other group chats. Messages friends about their travel plans. Compares Uber and Lyft prices. Sometimes changes or cancels plans because of transportation costs.

Feels

Frustrated by expensive transportation. Anxious about finding a ride at the right time. Uncertain about traveling with unfamiliar students. Relieved when someone shares the ride and the cost.

Alternative solutions

Three alternatives we considered, next to the one we chose.

  1. A. Ride board with departure windows (chosen)

    Students post a trip and see who else is leaving around the same time. The exact pickup spot appears after a request.

  2. B. Structured group chat process

    One pinned template per destination and week, posted at a fixed time in an existing group chat.

  3. C. Campus transit change

    Ask the university and student government for later and airport shuttles that match the same time windows.

We also considered an AI matching agent (option D). We set it aside because calendar access is a privacy barrier and there is no matching data yet.
