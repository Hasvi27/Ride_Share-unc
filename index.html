How it works

Three steps. Nobody pays us, and nobody books a driver.

  1. 1

    Post a trip

    Where you are going, the area you leave from, the date, a leave-by time, and the solo fare you would pay.

  2. 2

    Someone asks to join

    A student with a unc.edu address sends a ride-along request. You see their first name and the number of seats left.

  3. 3

    Split the fare

    The exact pickup spot appears once they request. Everyone pays their share directly to the driver's app.

The pickup spot shows up after a request

Only students who ask to ride see the exact meeting place. Everyone else sees the general area.

    Built for the way students actually travel

    Departure windows

    Post a leave-by time and see who else is leaving around then, not a vague "sometime this week."

    Clear cost split

    Every trip shows the solo fare and the per-person share before anyone asks to join.

    Exact spot after a request

    The meeting place stays hidden until a student asks to ride, so posts do not reach strangers' doorsteps.

    unc.edu only

    Posting and requesting need a unc.edu address. We check the domain only, so it is a first filter, not proof of identity.

    Filter by destination

    RDU, Southpoint, Raleigh, Durham, Charlotte, or anything else. Filters stay on the same page.

    Works on your phone

    Post and browse from a phone between classes. The layout adjusts to the screen you have.
