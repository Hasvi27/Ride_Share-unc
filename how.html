How it works

Three steps, no drivers, no payments.

Ride Share is a board for trips students are already planning. We do not book rides or handle money.

The steps

  1. Post the trip

    Destination, the area you leave from, the date, a leave-by time, the solo fare you expect, and how many seats you have.

  2. A student asks to ride along

    They need a unc.edu address. You see their first name and how many seats are left.

  3. Split the fare and go

    The exact pickup spot appears once they request. Each person pays their share directly, using whatever app or method the group agrees on.

The pickup spot shows after a request

Everyone sees the general area. The exact meeting place is only shown to students who ask to ride.

What the survey found

83 UNC students answered "Getting Around Without a Car at UNC." Question-level counts are in brackets because skip logic changed who answered what.

  • Have tried to find a shared ride 71 of 8385.5%
  • Asked a friend directly for a ride 68 of 7491.9%
  • Skipped or changed a trip because of cost 50 of 8339.8%
  • Named mismatched timing as a barrier 36 of 64 who named barriers56.3%
  • Named schedules not lining up 33 of 64 who named barriers51.6%
  • Did not know who else was going 24 of 6437.5%

Some answers mixed per-person and total costs, and a few outliers were kept and flagged. Ask us before quoting a single number.
